## Description  
_1595/4000 characters_

```
Homey Store is the Homey App Store the way you know it from your iPhone: clear, fast and beautiful. Browse every app for Homey Pro and Homey Cloud, see what they do and install them on your own Homey with a single tap.

DISCOVER
• Today: featured, popular and new apps on big cards
• Apps: top charts per category – lighting, energy, security, climate and more
• Search as you type, or paste a homey.app link to open an app directly
• Filter by Zigbee, Z-Wave, Matter, Bluetooth, 433 MHz, infrared, Wi-Fi and apps with widgets

EVERYTHING ABOUT AN APP
• Ratings and reviews, including the developer's response
• What's new and the full version history
• Supported devices with product photos, plus its widgets and Flow cards
• More apps from the same developer

INSTALL ON YOUR HOMEY
• Pick your Homey and install the live or the test version
• Update apps, switch between test and live, or roll back to an older version (Homey Pro)
• Works with Homey Pro, Homey Pro mini, Homey Self-Hosted Server and Homey Cloud

MY HOMEY
• All your installed apps in one place, with one-tap updates
• Automatic updates on or off and restart apps (Homey Pro)
• See right away which apps have crashed
• Your own apps as a developer, even when they only exist as a test

AND MORE
• Multiple Homey accounts with quick switching
• Light and dark, iPhone and iPad
• Browse first without signing in

Homey Store is an independent app and is not made by or affiliated with Athom B.V. Homey is a trademark of Athom B.V. App data comes from the public Homey App Store; installing happens through your own Homey account.
```
